Cross Victory Church

Founded on the apostles’ teaching

When Bishop Mbendera left the Church of England — and set aside a full–time musical journey with two record deals in South Africa — it was not for pride or provision. He felt called to start a full–time ministry following the teachings of the holy Apostles. Cross Victory Church began in the living room of his home.

Doctrine

We strictly follow the doctrine of the holy Apostles. We gather and worship on Saturdays, with an extra mid–week service and evangelism programs reaching the masses.

Baptism

Baptism by immersion, in the name of Jesus Christ. We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit and spiritual gifts — tongues handled exactly as the Apostles handled them.

Music

Worship music, hymns and spiritual songs — a ministry birthed from a lifetime of musicianship offered to the Lord.

“Feel free to connect with us. We are open, humble and caring.”
